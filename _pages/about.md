I am an M.S.–Ph.D. student at the [**High-Throughput Computer Research Center**](https://ict.cas.cn/jssgk/zzjg/kyxt/gtljsj/), [**Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences**](https://english.ict.cas.cn/). I completed the Hua Xia Talent Program in Computer Science and Technology at the [**University of Science and Technology of China**](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/), where I received dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Technology and Finance in 2024.

My research focuses on **computer architecture and edge AI computing**, particularly the efficient execution of large language models and 3D Gaussian Splatting. My work spans domain-specific accelerators, dataflow architectures, processing-in-memory, and algorithm–architecture co-design. I also develop RISC-V processors and supporting toolchains and contribute to open-source hardware and computer architecture education. In 2026, I was selected for the Young Science and Technology Talent Development Program (Special Program for Doctoral Students) of the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST), with training and mentorship provided by the China Computer Federation (CCF). I also received the Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis Award from the Anhui Computer Federation.

I have authored or coauthored six papers published or accepted at MICRO, ISCA, DAC, and related journals, including two as first author. My first-author work SCARF received all three MICRO 2026 artifact evaluation badges, and our collaborative work MLX was nominated for the ISCA 2026 Best Paper Award. I also serve on the MICRO 2026 Artifact Evaluation Committee. Please feel free to contact me about research collaboration, open-source hardware projects, or computer architecture education.

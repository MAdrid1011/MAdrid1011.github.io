CLAP implements a dual-issue, eight-stage in-order CPU supporting the LoongArch 32-bit reduced instruction set, together with cache, prefetching, branch prediction, and TLB modules.

The complete system boots Linux at 80 MHz on a Xilinx FPGA platform and placed second in the LoongArch track of the National Student Computer System Capability Challenge.
